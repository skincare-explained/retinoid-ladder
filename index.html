The Retinoid Ladder

Retinal vs retinol, from the reel. Find your stage, then the products and routine for it.

Why it matters
Retinol
retinol → retinal → retinoic acid
2 steps
Retinal
retinal → retinoic acid
1 step

Your skin can only use retinoic acid. One fewer step makes retinal faster and stronger, and more likely to cause redness, flaking or stinging.

Skin type guide
Dry

Try the sandwich method: moisturizer, then retinoid, then moisturizer again. A ceramide cream helps. Stay at Stage 1 a little longer.

Sensitive

Start with 1 night a week. Patch test along the jaw for 3 nights first. A low-dose encapsulated retinol is the kindest start.

Oily or acne-prone

You can often move up sooner. Use a light gel moisturizer, and skip AHA or BHA exfoliants on retinoid nights.

Combination

Use the full amount on oily areas and less on dry cheeks. Add more moisturizer where your skin feels tight.

Small breakouts in your usual spots during the first 4–6 weeks can be purging. Burning, swelling or a rash in new places means stop and step back.

Before you start
Skip retinoids, or ask a doctor first, if
You are pregnant, breastfeeding, or trying to conceive.
You have eczema, rosacea, or a skin condition being treated.
You use a prescription retinoid like tretinoin or adapalene. Follow your doctor's plan.
